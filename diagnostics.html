Recovering from the unknown state

The reader enters unknown when the RFID module does not confirm a Stop, a power write or another command, or sends data the firmware does not expect. It sends a health notification with the reason. Firmware 0.5.0 keeps this state until power is removed.

  1. Call status.get and save the whole reply: reason, session, input_errors and unexpected_frame when present.
  2. Treat the last session as incomplete. If an EPC write was in progress, plan to scan that tag by TID after recovery.
  3. Remove every power source of the reader (PoE or DC, and USB), then power it again.
  4. Reconnect and call hello. Confirm that boot_id has changed, then poll status.get until state is idle.
  5. Apply again every setting that was not saved in the module, such as power set with reader.set_config, or any setting written with persist=false. Read the settings back before the next Start.

If the state returns to unknown after a power cycle, send the saved status.get replies to Nextwaves support.

-32002reader_not_idleWait for status.get.state = idle and send the request again. In unknown, recover first.
-32003power_limitedLower the power to hello.power_limit_dbm or less. For Start and write_epc, first lower the cached power of the selected antenna.
-32005idempotency_conflictUse a new UUID for every new action. Reuse a UUID only to repeat the identical request.
-32006operation_history_full, module_history_fullWait and retry. An entry is released 60000 ms after its operation ended. Keep below 64 inventory sessions and 32 module writes per minute.

-32008Network and firmware messagesRead message, then ota.status or network.get. maintenance_busy clears when the upload ends or the boot check completes.

Data completeness criteria

A session delivered all its data when every check below passes.

CheckWhere to read it
outcome is successThe last inventory.state, or status.get.session.
The reader state is idlestatus.get.
Every seq from 1 to last_seq arrivedCount them in your client. The SDK reports the result as DataComplete.
reported_drops is 0 and input_errors is 0The session object.

The reader queues up to 32 tag messages for the controller connection, on WSS and on USB COM alike. When the controller reads too slowly the queue fills, reported_drops increases and the outcome becomes partial. In the SDK a full event buffer closes the connection. Store the final session status together with the tags you captured.

Complete data means that everything the RFID module reported reached your application. It does not mean that every tag near the antenna was read. Check tag coverage with a known set of tags.

Disconnect and retry policy

SituationWhat to do
The connection closed during a sessionReconnect, call hello and compare board_id and boot_id. Read status.get to see how the session ended. Tags sent while you were disconnected are not sent again.
No reply to inventory.startSend the same request again with the same op_id. The reader keeps a request for at least 60 seconds within one boot and does not start a second session.
No reply to a setter or to write_epcCall module.result with the same op_id. After an uncertain EPC write, scan the tag before you write again.
boot_id changedThe reader restarted and no longer remembers earlier requests. Read the settings back and apply again the ones that were not saved.
A Gateway client lost its connectionContinue from the last cursor you saved. A gap event, or a stream_quality other than complete, shows that data is missing.

Stop a session with inventory.stop and wait for finished before lease.release or disconnecting. A session ended by lease release, lease expiry or disconnection is not followed by a final inventory.state, and reports still arriving while RF stops are counted in reported_drops.

Frequently asked questions

QuestionAnswer
Can two applications control one reader at the same time?No. A reader serves one authenticated controller connection. A second WSS connection gets HTTP 503 and USB gets reader_busy. Let several applications share a reader through the Gateway.
Does the reader keep tags while my application is disconnected?No. When the connection closes the reader stops RF, and tag messages are not stored. Use the Gateway journal when you need replay.
Do I have to send lease.renew myself?The SDK and the Gateway renew automatically. A custom client sends it about every 1000 ms.
Where do I set Q, session and target?With the set_baseband operation while the reader is idle. They are not parameters of inventory.start.
Is antenna power kept after a power cycle?Power set with reader.set_config, or with set_power and persist=false, is not saved. set_power with persist=true asks the RFID module to save it. The reader cannot check that the module kept the value, so read the power back after a power cycle when you commission the installation.
Does outcome success mean every tag was read?No. It means everything the RFID module reported was delivered. Coverage of the physical tags depends on antenna placement, power and time, and is checked with a known tag set.
